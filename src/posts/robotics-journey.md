My journey into robotics started with a competition and led to published research. Here's the story.

## RoboCup China Open 2024

The **2024 China Robot Competition & RoboCup China Open** was my first major robotics competition. Our team built a basketball simulation robot that competed autonomously.

### Technical Challenges

- **Radar data processing** — Real-time field environment acquisition
- **Camera tracking** — Visual landmark detection and position tracking
- **PID control** — Motion optimization for precise movement
- **Sensor filtering** — Noise reduction for accurate perception

After months of iteration, our team won the **National First Prize** (Champion).

## From Competition to Research

The competition experience naturally led to research. I joined a project on **structural health monitoring for aerospace composites**, working on:

### CFRP Bolt Damage Detection

Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymers (CFRP) are widely used in aerospace, but detecting damage in bolted joints is challenging. We developed:

- **Embedded CNT eddy current sensors** integrated into the bolt structure
- **Real-time damage self-diagnosis** capability
- A **patented fabrication process** for smart sensing bolts

This work resulted in a publication in **Polymer Composites** (IF: 4.7) and a **granted national invention patent**.

## What I Learned

- Real-world systems are noisy — filtering and signal processing are essential
- Research and competition engineering reinforce each other
- Sensor fusion (radar + camera + eddy current) is a recurring theme in my work
- Building things that actually work in the real world is deeply satisfying
